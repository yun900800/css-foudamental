Simple Omelette Recipe

    Prepare time

  • Preparation Time: 10 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 2

    Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese (optional)

    instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  2. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add the butter.
  3. Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the egg mixture.
  4. Cook for 2-3 minutes, gently stirring with a spatula until the eggs are set but still slightly runny.
  5. If using cheese, sprinkle it on one half of the omelette.
  6. Fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute until the cheese is melted.
